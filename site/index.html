Independent study by Nikhil Sehgal, using public data only. Not a product of Daimler Truck, the University of St.Gallen, BMW Group, Ulm University or Catena-X. Proof of concept. Illustrative backtests of single cases, not forecasts.

Days of warning

How many days of warning is a hidden-tier supply chain map worth? Each case compares an observer who knows only the public map with an observer who knows the full map.

Cases

The 2025 Nexperia chip disruption

How many days of warning was a hidden-tier map worth in the 2025 Nexperia disruption?

First stop: 2025-10-28. Map value: 330 days. Generic variant: 0 days.
